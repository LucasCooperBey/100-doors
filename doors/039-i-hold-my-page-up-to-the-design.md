## What it is

I hold my page up to the design before I call it done. Small gaps are easy to miss by eye. visual-verdict compares a screenshot with its reference image and lists specific fixes.

It needs a reference image and a screenshot. Its scores are model judgments, not measurements. It is adapted from Yeachan Heo's oh-my-claudecode, MIT licensed; the skill's Source section says what changed.
