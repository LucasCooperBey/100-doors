## What it is

I like hearing what people remember from a song. banger-recall is my take on someone else's idea from the Slopathon, the hackathon Matthew Zimmer puts on alongside his Vibecraft meetups. A spoken check-in asks what stuck and compares it with the song brief.

It runs locally, not by phone. Live scoring needs an API key. The included replay samples are synthetic.
