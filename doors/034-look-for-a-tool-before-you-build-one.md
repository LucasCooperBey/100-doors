## What it is

Building is the fun part, which makes it easy to remake something that already exists. deep-recon sends research agents out in parallel to find existing solutions, read what people who tried them say and check the docs, then writes one research brief with a recommendation and the drawbacks of each alternative.

It needs web research and multi-agent tools. A research brief template is included in the folder.
