## What it is

Every loaded tool and instruction takes space before the real work starts. context-budget estimates the tokens your skills, agents, MCP tools, rules and hooks load, so you can see what to switch off.

The numbers are estimates, not exact token counts. It does not switch anything off for you. The idea came from Affaan Mustafa's everything-claude-code; the skill's Source section says so.
