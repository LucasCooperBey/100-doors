## What it is

A paste-in prompt that turns a video transcript into shorter flowing prose you can listen to.

The public gift is the prompt from YT-Condense, a private bookmarklet first committed June 9, 2026. It works in any chat with no code. Paste a video's transcript, ask for flowing prose aimed at about a fifth of the length, then paste the result into a text-to-speech reader. The video needs captions.
