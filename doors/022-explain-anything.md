## What it is

A skill that explains anything at four altitudes, plain words first and engineering last.

It opens with one sentence a stranger gets, then goes down: 100,000 feet for the plain point, 50,000 for where it fits, 30,000 for its shape in plain-text diagrams, and 10,000 for the engineering. Each level is readable on its own, so anyone can stop where they have what they need.
