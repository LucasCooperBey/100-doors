## What it is

Skills and rules pile up, and two of them can quietly ask for opposite things. ecosystem-audit reviews your Claude Code setup and returns a ranked list of overlaps and conflicts. It fans out many agents and deletes nothing. The idea came from Mark Kashef's Dynamic Workflows prompt pack; the skill's Source section says so.

It needs access to the setup you want reviewed.
