## What it is

I ask what the task left behind. A job can finish while a loose end or a risk slips by. whatdidimiss is a skill you run after a task. It lists real follow-ups, and it reports nothing when nothing genuine came up.

It reflects on the work just done. It is not a fresh audit and it creates no tasks on its own.
