## What it is

Six months from now you will look at something you built and wonder why you did it that way. The work shows what you chose. It never shows why, or what you turned down.

A dated decision note fixes that: one short file per decision, with the date, what you picked, what you passed on, and the reason.

## Try it today

**Zero-code prompt (paste into ChatGPT or Claude after any big choice):**

```text
Turn what I just decided into a dated decision note: today's date, the decision in one sentence, every option I considered with a one-line reason I passed on it, and what would make me revisit it.
```
