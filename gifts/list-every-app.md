# List every app you started

By mid-2026 one of my accounts held 38 apps. My own count had gone from 14 to 24 to 38 as I looked.

## The exercise (15 minutes)

1. List every app, script and project you have started. Open each tool you build in, plus your GitHub, and copy the names. Don't judge yet.
2. Next to each, write one word: **works**, **broken** or **unknown**.
3. Pick one. The best pick is one you still think about.
4. Write one sentence: "To be useful to one person today, it needs ___."
5. If the blank is small enough to finish this week, put it on your calendar. If it isn't, write down why and cross the app off.

## What usually fills the blank

- A real user named in the sentence instead of "people".
- One feature removed, so the one that matters works.
- Real data in place of sample data.
- A link someone can open without your login.

## Try it

Do steps 1 to 4 now. Count your list. The number alone tells you something about how you build.
