# A blank intake form

My first form app started from a 1,015-line questionnaire attached to its first commit. 15 minutes later it was a form you could open from a link. A form fits on a phone and asks one thing at a time, which a long document does not. Here is a blank one to copy into any form tool (Google Forms, Tally, Typeform or the one you already use) before you start a project with someone: a friend's website, a club's sign-up page, a favor for a neighbor.

Keep only the questions you will act on. Every extra question costs you answers.

## The form

**Before you start**
- Intro line at the top of the form: "This takes about five minutes. Skip anything that doesn't apply."

**1. About you**
- Your name (short answer, required)
- Best way to reach you (choice: text, email, call) and the number or address (short answer)
- Best days and times to reach you (short answer)

**2. What you need**
- In one sentence, what do you want to exist when we're done? (short answer, required)
- Who is it for? (short answer)
- What do you use today instead, and what's wrong with it? (paragraph)

**3. What good looks like**
- How will you know it worked? Name one thing you could count or check. (short answer)
- Is there a date it has to be ready by? Why that date? (short answer)

**4. What you already have**
- Links, photos, logins you'll share later, documents (file upload or paragraph)
- Anything that must stay the same, like a name, colors or a tool your team already uses (paragraph)

**5. Limits**
- Budget, if there is one (choice: none yet, under a set amount you'll name, we'll talk)
- Anything that must not happen, such as a public launch before you approve it (paragraph)

**6. Permission**
- May I keep your answers to work on this project? (yes or no, required)
- May I mention the project in public once it's done? (choice: yes, ask me first, no)

**Closing line:** "Thanks. I'll read this and reply within [two days]."

## Try it

Take one questionnaire you send around as a document or a long message. Rebuild it from the sections above, cut every question you would not act on, and send the link to the next person instead. Compare how long the answers take to come back.

## Keep it safe

Never ask for a password, a card number or an ID number in a form like this. If you need access to something, ask for it in person or through the tool's own sharing settings.
